Hi, I'm Sanad 👋

🎓 About Me: I am an Assistant Professor of Data Science in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Sam Houston State University. I earned my Ph.D. in Data Science & Analytics from Kennesaw State University. My research sits at the intersection of statistical inference, machine learning, explainability, and modern AI systems.

🔍 Current Work: My current projects focus on predictive modeling, feature significance and explainable AI, temporal validation of machine learning models, generative AI and retrieval-augmented generation, and reproducible ML workflows. I am especially interested in methods that are accurate, interpretable, and useful in real-world decision settings.

📚 Teaching: I teach undergraduate and graduate data science courses that integrate statistics, Python, R, machine learning, data management, visualization, and end-to-end analytics workflows.
