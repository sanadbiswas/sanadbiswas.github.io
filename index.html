Hi, I'm Sanad 👋

About Me. I am an Assistant Professor of Data Science in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Sam Houston State University. I earned my Ph.D. in Data Science & Analytics from Kennesaw State University.

My research focuses on predictive modeling, statistical inference, machine learning systems, explainable AI, fairness, generative AI, retrieval-augmented generation, and MLOps. I am particularly interested in building models that are accurate, interpretable, reproducible, and useful in real-world decision-making.
