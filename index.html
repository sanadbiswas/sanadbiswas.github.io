About

Data science research with statistical depth and practical impact.

I am an Assistant Professor of Data Science in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Sam Houston State University. I received my Ph.D. in Data Science and Analytics from Kennesaw State University.

My research focuses on predictive modeling, statistical inference, machine learning systems, explainability and feature importance, fairness, generative AI, retrieval-augmented generation, and MLOps. I am particularly interested in developing methods that are not only accurate, but also interpretable, reproducible, and useful in real-world decision-making settings.
